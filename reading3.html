Reading #3 Memunatu

On the surface the illustrations are used as a way to connect the words in the story to a picture that gives an easily immersive experience in comparison to raw imagination when reading an article. Though I do think the illustrations give the chance to view the story under the light that Memunatu experienced; this includes the kind of mood set by the colors, angles, and facial expressions, depicted in a way Memunatu wanted. This makes it personal and easier to connect to the story on a level that plain words can’t always match. The illustrations aren’t actually far different from the photographs of the hospital but they do give it a different kind of life and emotion than the photos do. The illustrations in this scenario are better at conveying the emotions experienced by Memunatu than I think pictures ever could because pictures can only capture what is seen directly in front of you; everything past that is an assumption that illustrations can take and turn into a comprehensible visual.
