Social Campaign Reflection

With unlimited access to the internet and people on the internet, the world has become connected without a clock on it. Attention is easy to expect from people, knowing they are two taps away from responding to you. The expectations set for humans, yet young adults especially, by social media have you in a never-ending race of “self-improvement” and progression, if you will. We are too afraid to be ourselves and often times try to hold ourselves to unreasonable standards set through comparison in the never-ending world of social media. I find a lot of the relationships I have, both romantic and platonic, to feel often synthetic the more accepting we become of faking it instead of presenting our raw flawed selves.
